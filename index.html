Frequently asked questions

How do I save my picture?

Tap Done · Save to Photos, pick a size (Original, 4:5, 1:1 or Story 9:16) and tap Save to Photos. The first time, allow “Add Photos Only” so the app can save to your library.

Can I use the same design on many photos?

Yes. Save it in Frame → My Styles to reuse any time, or tap Done → Use this design on more photos to frame up to 20 photos at once.

Can I use my own logo?

Yes. In Frame → Signature mark, choose My logo and pick a picture. You can also hide the mark.

How do I change the language?

The app follows your iPhone language. You can also pick English, 简体中文, 繁體中文 or Bahasa Melayu on the start screen.

The camera details are empty

Screenshots and photos sent through chat apps often lose their camera data. Open the Camera tab and type the details in — or switch any line off.

Can I move and resize the text, camera details and stickers?

Yes. Drag anything on the picture. Pinch with two fingers to resize and twist to rotate. Sliders are in each tab too.

Beauty can’t find a face

Use a photo where faces are clear, well lit and facing the camera. Tap Find faces again in the Beauty tab. Body shaping still works without a face.

Does the app upload my photos?

No. Everything — including face detection — happens on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy.

I bought the app on another iPhone

Sign in with the same Apple ID and download it again from the App Store at no extra cost.

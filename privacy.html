Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Short version: Your Moment Frame does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Your photos and everything you do in the app stay on your iPhone.

Photos

Photos you choose are opened and edited only on your device to create your framed picture. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Camera details

Camera information (such as phone model, lens and camera settings) is read from the photo’s own metadata on your device and is used only to draw it on your picture. You can edit or hide any detail.

Face detection and beauty tools

When you use the Beauty tools (makeup, skin smoothing, face and body shaping), the app detects faces in the photo you are editing so the effects land in the right place. This uses Apple's Vision framework, which is built into iOS and runs entirely on your iPhone — nothing about your photos or your faces, and no usage statistics, is sent to us, to Apple or to anyone else. Face information:

  • is processed only on your device, only for the photo you are editing;
  • is never stored after you close the photo, and never uploaded or shared;
  • is not used to identify anyone, and not used for tracking, analytics or advertising.

Saving and sharing

The app saves your finished picture to your Photos library, and shares it only when you choose to share it.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

There is no sign-up and no login. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking code, and it does not use your data to track you across other apps or websites.

Permissions

  • Photos — to pick the photos you want to frame, and to save the result.
  • Camera — only if you choose “Take a photo”.

You can change these at any time in Settings › Your Moment Frame on your iPhone.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
